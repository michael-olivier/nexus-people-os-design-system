Warning Messages

Warnings flag risk before someone loses work, access, or data. Be clear about consequences and distinguish “you can proceed” from “you cannot proceed.”

Writing guidelines

  • Title states the risk (“You’ll lose unsaved edits”).
  • Body explains impact and time sensitivity if relevant.
  • Actions: destructive on the left or secondary; safe default emphasized.
  • Don’t use warnings for routine info—that trains people to ignore them.

Examples

You’ll lose unsaved edits

Leave this page and your changes to the growth goals section won’t be saved.

Review access before you share

People outside your team may see salary band summaries in this export.

Do / Don’t

Do

Deleting this plan removes checkpoints for your direct reports.

Don’t

Warning: Are you sure? This action may or may not have effects.
