Success Messages

Success messages confirm that something completed. Stay brief and calm—save enthusiasm for human moments, not routine saves.

Writing guidelines

  • Title names what succeeded (“Development plan saved”).
  • Optional body: what’s next or where to find the result.
  • No exclamation marks in UI flags.
  • Auto-dismiss short confirmations; keep persistent banners for actions that need follow-up.

Examples

Development plan saved

Share it with your manager when you’re ready for review.

Feedback request sent

We’ll notify you when your peer responds.

Do / Don’t

Do

Profile updated.

Don’t

Awesome job!!! You did it!!! 🎉
